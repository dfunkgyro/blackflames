## 2. Characters

Narrators in this part (all first person unless noted): the Entity/Schism (2862–2887, 2913–2916, 2968–2970); Alfonso V (2920–2966, 3006–3040, then as a ghost inside the entity in 3066–3112, 3350–3536); Queen Isabella of Castile (2974–3005); Catalina (3044–3062, unnamed in-section); collective blackamoor "we" (3116); Isabella daughter of Catalina (3120–3148, 3252–3346); unnamed exile with Alaric/Kofi/Yusuf (3150–3210); Elias (3212–3248); Tariq (3540–3624); Diego Velasquez (3628–3658); Torquemada, puppeted by the God of Chaos (3664–3704). ### The Entity / the Schism / the shadow entity / God of Chaos / "Masonic Guild of Fascism"
- First appearance in range: 2862 (born as the schism, earlier bound into Aymeric 2842).
- Role: primordial antagonist; narrator of its own birth. Woven from witches' rites, masons' carvings, monks' chants, "Christian and Muslim prayers".
- Traits: formless, hungry without a mouth, amoral ("I did not know good or evil; I understood only hunger", 2866), "keeper of thresholds" (2872), feeds on division, conquest, slavery, erasure. Gains "consciousness" twice (1130 and 1190).
- Wants: to grow; to merge magic and technology.
- Secrets: is fed unknowingly by lodges (Lisbon lodge 2970) and papal bulls; may be the hidden author of papal commands ("the elusive entity behind the mask of the Pope", 3638).
- Fate: ascendant; "wears many faces: Inquisition, Reformation, Crown Proclamation" (3080).

### Aymeric (context, 2778–2844)
Cardinal/cleric who bound the Schism into his own body; "the vessel for the dark entity" (2842). Body left in the Basilica. Not mentioned again in range — key dangling thread.

### Pope Innocent II (2870)
Named as bowing to rituals as the entity forms. Historical (1130 schism with Anacletus II).

### Pope Alexander VI / Rodrigo Borgia (2891 "Aleander", 2895, 2998, 3004)
Issues Inter Caetera (May 1493); his cardinals build the Papal Bull monolith. As a Valencian cardinal he facilitated the forged Pius II dispensation for the White Flame; resented the deception; named Isabella & Ferdinand Catholic Monarchs. Secret: complicit in a forgery from a dead pope's name.

## 3. Lore and magic

- **The Entity's birth (the Schism).** "I was just the schism within the church" (2862); "Entity with only the remnants of rituals" (2862); woven from "stone circles, moonlit chants, fires lit under the stars" (2862); "The witches had known me first" (2872); "I was the spirit of the in-between, the keeper of thresholds" (2872); formed from "masonry, witchcraft, Christian and Muslim prayers" (2878); "neither god nor demon" (2880); hunger without a mouth (2866). Self-naming: "You can call me Masonic Guild of Fascism" (2886).
- **Technology prophecy.** "rituals will find their way into circuits and sigils into data" (2884). A far-future thread (tie to any modern-era books).
- **A second birth.** 1190: "the clash of worlds ignited my consciousness" (2913), and it feasts on "their desire for dominance" (2916).
- **Papal bulls as conduits.** Dum Diversas words "seemed to shift and change in the moonlight" (2950). The bull is "stained with salt spray and blood" (2960). Inter Caetera as a literal machine: "a towering monolith that would draw energy from the heavens" (2893); ash that corrupts rather than cleanses (2895).
- **Lodges as unknowing altars.** Lisbon lodge: "an unknowing altar to my growing power" (2970). Contrast: the Moor lodges (3295ff, 3538ff), counter-lodges of survival with signs, oaths and the crescent ring. Kamal: "We are masons now" (3606). This sets up a mirror between masonry that feeds the entity and masonry that resists it.
- **The forged dispensation.** A dead pope's seal, granted by favours "from this world or the next" (2988). The White Flame Order wants to bind Spain to the Church.
- **The covenant / shadow-merge.** Three rulers' shadows merge into a "unified shadow entity" (3022, 3028). "Our dark covenant" persists across faiths: "Different faith, same darkness" (3084); "needs no Pope's blessing now" (3100).
- **Vessels.** Aymeric is the vessel of the bound Schism (2842). James is "The covenant had found its new vessel" (3396). Bancroft: "God's chosen vessel" (3438), which echoes it. Torquemada is a face the God of Chaos wears (3664).
- **Ghost witness.** Alfonso watches "through death and beyond" (3066) from inside the entity and "weeps" (3110, 3406).
- **Crescent-moon sigils.** Bow mark (3140), pendant (3299), rings (3314, 3542), pass phrase "La luna creciente brilla en la noche oscura / Y guía a los viajeros perdidos a casa" (3305–3306), oath "By the light of the crescent moon, by the memory of Granada" (3328). These are presented as mundane, but they could serve as wards against the entity.
- **Curses / prophecies.** Duarte's warning (2942) is proto-prophetic. "What we had unleashed would echo through centuries" (2966). Chaos: "You gave them the blueprint for annihilation" (3704). The entity's own prophecy: "The world will hear me... And the world will change" (2887).
- **Cardinals.** Collective cardinals builders of the monolith (2893); Cardinal Almeida (2922); Borgia as a Valencian cardinal (2998); Mendoza (historical cardinal); Cardinal Borghese (3275).
- **Absent:** black flames, white flames as magic (only the "Order of the White Flame"), Ark/fragments, Anti-Entity.

## 7. Untapped potential

- **The White Flame Order** vs the Blackflames: make the "White Flame" the Church's secret order of fire-priests (white flames = sanctified inquisitorial pyres that feed the Schism), mirroring the protagonists' black flames. It forged the dispensation and intercepts decrees (3040), so it can be the series' transnational secret police, operating covertly even in Protestant England (fixing the "Inquisition in Greenwich" error).
- **Aymeric's body as vessel**: where is it entombed? The 1190 "second awakening" could be its seal cracking. A relic hunt for Aymeric's crucifix (a fragment of the Ark?) is possible.
- **Who signs for the dead pope?** The forgery could be literal necromancy: the White Flame can make the dead sign. A puzzle-box mystery.
- **The crescent tokens as wards**: each token is unique and "cannot be copied" (3322). Make them the first counter-magic: the Schism cannot see bearers, which is why erasure must be done by human betrayers (Thomas Moore, Safiya's lover).
- **The two lodges**: Kamal's men-only cells vs Elena/Isabella's women-led crescent sisterhood. A political schism among the resisters echoes the Church's schism; the entity feeds on it too.
- **"Grand Architect" stolen**: reveal that later Freemasonry appropriated Moor-lodge rites, and the Schism re-enters through that appropriation, closing the loop with its birth from "masons" (2872).
- **Duarte's fate**: the conscience who saw the shadow first could become a defector, an early Anti-Entity agent.
- **Abena's fate**: follow her across the Atlantic; her descendants carry the story to the mutiny ship that "carried proof back" (3204). A long-arc payoff in the abolition book.
- **Elias's testimony**: an embedded document passed down to Clarkson, the testimony itself as the weapon that wounds the entity, since the entity feeds on erasure and is starved by remembrance ("Cuida nuestra historia"). This gives the magic system a rule and a cost: memory as counter-magic.
- **Beatriz in Cardinal Borghese's household**: a spy inside Rome; letters as a POV thread.
- **Isabella named for the queen**: a character confronting her name, perhaps renaming herself Aasma after her mother's lost name.
- **Catalina's secret about Arthur's wedding night**: a political bomb the White Flame wants. Her silence protected Catherine, and the secret could pass down in the ledger.
- **James's hunger for demonology**: James could sense the entity and try to *control* it (Daemonologie as a grimoire in disguise). The skeptic who becomes its scholar is a fine antagonist POV.
- **Caspar van Senden** (the real 1596 Lübeck merchant licensed to deport Black people from England) is an ideal grey antagonist for an Elizabethan chapter.
- **Rosa and Zoser**: re-anchor them in this era. Rosa's designs used to build the "monolith" bull; Zoser trying to save "the tribes" among the Limba and Mane would connect the frame protagonists to the 1596 exile thread.
- **Alfonso's redemption arc**: can the ghost inside the entity betray it from within? A long-game twist would be the narrator-witness as the entity's flaw.
