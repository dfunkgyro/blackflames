# THE BLACKFLAMES — Mystery Ledger

**Rules (audit §3I):**
1. Every major reveal has **at least three fair clues** planted in earlier chapters.
2. Every book closes at least one mystery and opens another.
3. No POV chapter lies. Hidden information is withheld by **cuts and omissions**, never by false narration.
4. Every reveal changes how an earlier scene reads.

**Notation.** `I·7` means Book I, Chapter 7 (Book I chapter numbers are binding: `books/book-01/PLAN.md`). For Books II–VII, chapter numbers are **provisional targets** given as `II·~20` and tied to the Part named in `series-outline.md`. They become binding when each book's PLAN is written. `K` means a Keeper's catalogue entry (`K-I open`, `K-III close`). `Pro` = Prologue; `Epi` = Epilogue.
