# THE BLACKFLAMES — Magic System: *The Word and the Wound*

Binding for all writers. Its sources are the bible (§2, §6), Proposal C's laws and visuals, Proposal A's costs, and Proposal B's final law, as synthesised in `series-outline.md`. Line references (`l.`) are to `blackflames.md`.

**Governing principle.** Magic is rare, dreadful, costly and half-understood. The characters never hold this document. They hold rumours, scripture, ledgers and scars. Everything below is the truth the writers know. Each book shows the reader only the slice listed in §12.

**On-page rule.** No law below is ever stated as an infodump. Readers learn a rule only when it costs someone something in a scene.

## 1. The Eight Laws

| # | Law | Grounding | On the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Word writes the world.** Text in the warden-script, text written with Ark wood, and text witnessed by a Rite all bind what they name. | ll.548, 1069, 1430, 2255, 2998 | Bulls, deeds, charters and translations are spell-documents. What they declare begins to come true in the bodies they name. |
| 2 | **What you swallow, you carry; what carries you takes your time.** | ll.620, 1057, 1716, 1730 | Ingesting a fragment or the Entity's substance makes a vessel. The vessel stops ageing and loses time. |
| 3 | **There is one fire, and it reads two ways.** It burns **black** in one who remembers and **white** in one who denies or commands. | ll.47, 977, 1035–1041; resolves bible OPEN-18 | Melted in Angeska's brazier under Rome's purpose, the Key goes gold, red, then **white**. In a remembering hand the same wood burns black. |
| 4 | **Erasure feeds the Wound; remembrance starves it.** | ll.3732–3752, 2767, 3526, 3146 | Every renaming, burned library and unrecorded deportation is a meal. Every testimony takes something away. |
| 5 | **Every division begets its negation.** | ll.4533, 4778, 4788 | The negation is born out of the Entity's own diet: the names of the chained, chanted in a hold. |
| 6 | **Names carry weight.** | ll.1371, 2415, 5736 | Renaming a person is a small white-flame act. Speaking a true name in public is a small black one. |
| 7 | **Compassion repels; conviction wards.** | ll.1596, 4584 | The Entity cannot hold a host against kindness (Bellini) and cannot touch someone of total purpose (Nanny). Both are protection, never weapons. |
| 8 | **Nothing is unwritten without fire, and every erasure leaves a scar.** | ll.2765–2767 | Erased facts leave contradictions in the record: the **Curator's Scars**. The manuscript's own continuity errors become designed, solvable clues. |

**The final law (withheld until Book VII).** The two flames are the fire on the mountain that burned and was not consumed. It **remembers when it is witnessed with love and erases when it is wielded as decree**. The Entity lives in the space between the two: the Wound (l.1526), filled with everything white fire has taken.

## 2. The two fires: visuals, effects, costs, limits

| | **Black flame** (*Lux Obscura*, "dark light") | **White flame** (the Order's purifying fire) |
|---|---|---|
| **Look** | A flame of negative space, like a tongue of darkness cut out of the air, with a violet-black rim. Frost forms on its edges. Candles nearby lean *toward* it, turn blue and go out. Its smoke **falls before it rises**, then climbs and **writes** on ceilings and sails. It makes no sound. | Bright, smokeless, white-gold, with a faint hum like a struck glass. **Things lit by it cast no shadow.** |
| **Heat** | Cold. It can be held a moment by one who remembers. | Furnace-hot. Lead runs. |
| **Ash** | Grey ash that **settles into script**: the last true text of whatever burned (names, faces, sentences, a street plan). Legible forever to anyone who can read the warden-script, and in time to anyone at all (§12). | Fine white ash like salt or flour. It holds nothing, and the burned thing leaves no smell. |
| **Effect** | **Records.** It can also *seal* a text so that it cannot be forged or copied (the crescent tokens, l.3322). Writing made by it cannot be scraped off: the letters re-form under the knife. **Black-flame text about a living person is never finished.** It keeps writing for as long as that person lives: new words appear on Gebre's palm, and the Bet Maryam ceiling rearranges on the night of the Rite. | **Erases.** Witnesses within a few paces lose the memory of what burned. Archives "forget" the entries. A man burned white becomes "a heretic", with no name in any register. |
| **Feeds** | Starves the Entity. | Feeds the Entity, even when the wielder believes he serves God. |
| **Who can light it** | Only someone who **remembers who they are**. | Anyone who holds authority's seal (writ, bull, charter) and **commands**. |
| **Cost** | **The lighter's own memories**, most recent tender ones first, unchosen. Lit **collectively**, the cost spreads thin: found family is literally the cheapest way to burn black. If a person burns their **entire name**, they write themselves out of time: unseen, undying, unrecorded (Zaynab, 1492). | **The wielder's shadow and conscience.** Practised inquisitors slowly lose their shadows. Torquemada is "what's left of him" (l.3664); Diego has his "hollow ache". The wielder also becomes **unrememberable**: in the end their own mothers forget their faces. |
| **Limit** | It burns *completely* only on Ark acacia; on ordinary fuel it burns black only at the edges or core (Tamrat's pyre). It cannot be lit by the forgetful, which is why Zoser, who loses time, fails for centuries. | It cannot touch what black fire has already sealed. It can be fooled by a forgery it believes. |
| **Clever exploitation** | **The ledger technique** (Isabella, 1612): before a burn, write down the memory the fire will take, and read it back afterwards. The cipher ledger is literally a memory bank, and "*Cuida nuestra historia*" is an operating instruction. | **The shadow test**: the resisters spot Order agents by holding a lamp and watching for a missing shadow. The Order counters by carrying two lamps, so that the false second shadow passes. |

**The Key's melt.** In Angeska's brazier the Key goes gold, then red, then white, under the Rite's purpose. A remembering bearer would have burned it black. This is the first planted proof of the final law.

## 3. Writing as magic: documents as spells

- A sealed document **binds** when authority stands behind it **and** people comply with it. A document written with **the Nib** (Fragment 3) or pressed with **the Seal** (Fragment 5) binds harder: its clauses enter the bodies it names. The ink shows as a grey line under the skin that only vessels can see.
- A **forgery binds while it is believed** (the Pius II dispensation, 1469; the Company's deeds, c.1840).
- A document **breaks** when it is contradicted by a **truer, witnessed record**: a testimony, a counter-ledger, a name spoken in public, an annotation in a second hand. Every Curator's Scar is a breaking point.
- **The Curator edits; the resisters annotate.** The series' palimpsest form (§11) is the magic made visible.
- **Translation is the subtlest spell.** Whoever renders a text decides what it binds. Angeska softens; Beatrice mistranslates; Nafisa corrects. *Untranslated speech is never neutral.*

## 4. Vessels

**Made by ingestion and writing.** The fragment or the Entity's substance enters the body, and the body is written on.

**Rules (bible §6.4, retained):**
1. A vessel does not age while the Entity holds it, and loses time (time-blindness).
2. Two vessels' entities can merge and splinter into a child (Rosa, 1439).
3. A dying vessel's essence passes to the nearest vessel (Angeska → Rosa, l.1936).
4. A vessel can be bound by sacrifice of self (Haimeric, l.2840).
5. **Killing a vessel releases what it holds** (l.1898). This is the trap the last Humbert springs.
6. Vessels make flames falter and plants wither.
7. **[SHOWRUNNER RULING]** A vessel's skin carries the record of what it hosts; this is why Zoser's veins "rearrange". A vessel who **chooses** a black burning while remembering who he is **records** the Entity instead of releasing it. The body becomes a page (vellum). That is the series' endgame.

**The candle test.** Ordinary flame **leans toward** an unhidden fragment or vessel and **burns blue**. This is how the Silence-Keepers hunt bearers (Captain Humbert, Book I Ch.1). Fragment 0 and the crescent tokens defeat the test.

**Visual signatures of a vessel:** grey scales "like vellum"; ink-veins that move (l.1417); no taste and no warmth; a heartbeat out of step with the world (l.1379); plants wither and insects die nearby (l.1405); candles gutter; seers see "a relic-shaped hole" (l.1454).

## 5. The Ark and the fragments

The Ark is a **container for the Word**. Its acacia was cut "on the mountain where the fire spoke, before Moses came down" (bible §3.1), and it soaked up that fire. A splinter of it acts as a **wick**: a body, a pen or a box can hold the fire through it without being consumed. Every fragment is also a **seed** (l.1161) that can make a vessel. And the fragments are the only fuel on which black flame burns **completely**.

**Series midpoint turn (end of IV into V).** Hunting the fragments as *weapons* has fed the enemy, because every fragment found is a door. From then on they are gathered as **fuel**.

## 6. The Rite: one grammar

Every Rite has **five parts**:
1. a **Vessel**;
2. a **Crucible** (brazier, sarcophagus, Charter chest, conference table);
3. a **Splinter** or substance;
4. a **Reading**: a text spoken aloud;
5. **Seven Witnesses**, whose memory of the act is taken as a **tax**.

The rite at Avignon leaves seven witnesses amnesiac; the Clerk is the one who escapes the tax. Readers can **count witnesses** as a fair-play clue: seven cardinals at *Dum Diversas*; three crowns and four confessors at Alcáçovas; two times seven powers at Berlin, a **double Rite**.

An **anti-Rite** is a Reading that remembers instead of erasing, and needs no tax. Examples: Bois Caïman (1791); the Act of Independence read at Gonaïves (1804); the found family reading Zoser back to himself (1896). Every book's climax is a Rite or an anti-Rite.

| Haimeric's binding | II prologue | Haimeric / his own body / Nail / Latin exorcism / the Lateran conclave |
| The union, Florence 1438 | II | Angeska / a convent cell / the 1326 seed / her grimoire / seven nuns asleep |

## 7. Rosa's rule **[SHOWRUNNER RULING]**

- **Hunger.** About once a year she must take the last breath of a dying person, or her body ages toward its true age.
- **Inheritance.** She keeps every memory of everyone she consumes. She **cannot forget**, and she risks drowning in other lives. Her close third gathers italic intrusions from the dead (§11).
- **The tithe.** Every breakthrough of insight triggers a death near her that she did not choose. In Book III she deduces the pattern from the convent burial book.
- **Her ethic, earned.** From c.1500 she feeds only on the consenting dying. There is one lapse: Bengal, 1770, when she takes those not yet dying.
- **Her powers.** She reads the living script effortlessly; she has pattern-sight; she has "the silence of the owl, the subtlety of the cat, the patience of the spider" (l.2623). She can **set a black seal** on a document so that it cannot be white-burned or forged; this costs her one inherited life's memories.
- **Her end.** In Book VII she stops feeding, and ages.
- **She is the opposite of the Entity:** a body that consumes **and keeps**.

## 8. The Entity (the Wound): taxonomy and life cycle

**One being, many faces** (bible §6.3). Its faces are organs at different life stages, not separate gods.

| Face | What it is |
|---|---|
| The Curator | The archive face: it edits records, unmakes peoples, leaves scars. |
| The Schism | The division face: opened 1054, woken 1130, bound in Haimeric. |
| The God of Chaos | What its victims call it. It harvests *controlled* upheaval. |
| The Guild | Its second body from 1378: lodges, banks, trade. |
| The covenant | The pact rulers make with it; their shadows merge. |
| The Company | Its corporate mask, 1600–1874. |
| The General Act | Its last body, 1885. |

**Offspring.** Rosa's splintered inheritance (1439). The **Blood Thing** (1492; consumed by Rosa in 1503). The **winged being** (1783). The **noseless *ka*** of Giza (1798). Each is made of a specific erased group, and each can be named back.

**What it is (revealed in VII).** **The archive of the erased.** It is a wound filled with everything white fire has taken, monster and victim at once. Its hunger is the hunger of the erased to be recorded, twisted so that it can grow only by erasing others. Its oldest stratum is the **Il Moro**, unmade long ago by the Wardens of Aksum: the Wardens' secret guilt, and the root of Angeska's grey motive (to give the erased a body).

**Diet.** Division, conquest, enslavement as sacrifice, renaming, erasure.

## 9. The negation (it has no proper name)

- **Origin.** Born c.1615 in a slave ship's hold "between shackled souls" (l.4531), as captives chant their names. It is made of **names**, the same stuff as the Entity, turned the other way.
- **It has no body.** It works through volunteers (Nanny's people, Jay, Médard, Ayo, the Keepers). Each people names it in its own tongue; Jay calls it "Brother". Lady Liberty is the face empire gives it.
- **Its vessel's cost.** **They cannot stay.** Everyone Jay frees forgets his face.
- **Its limit.** "Ideas don't stand against maxim guns" (l.4449). It can also be **co-opted**: the 1739 treaty clause, the Black Companies sent against Fédon.

## 13. Forbidden in the prose

- No narrator explains a law. A character may *guess* one, and may be wrong.
- No spell without a price paid on the page in the same chapter or the next.
- No power appears without a prior plant. No power is used twice the same way without escalation or a new limit.
- No old power (obeah, Vodou, Kanu, Wardens) is framed as "dark magic". No darkening of skin is ever used as a sign of evil (E093). Angeska's change is material: char, glaze, ember-light.
- No modern magical vocabulary: "mana", "spell slots", "energy", "dimension", "aura". Characters use their own theological and craft vocabularies.
