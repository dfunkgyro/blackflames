Binding for every writer and every repair [R2-cont-02]. **Check every new chapter and every repair against this sheet at the moment of writing**, not against an older fix list. When a chapter fixes a new fact (a name, a date, an object's look, who speaks what to whom), the writer appends it under the right heading with the chapter number. Calendar arithmetic: Julian calendar throughout. Easter 1326 = Sunday 23 March (Paschal full moon Friday 21 March; the moon is **new about 6–7 March**, so Laetare week has no moonlight). Easter 1328 = 3 April; Ash Wednesday 1328 = 17 February.

## 5. Languages: who speaks what to whom

- Tamrat to Zoser: Amharic; Ge'ez in prayer and when he wants to be private in a Latin room.
- Angeska renders Ge'ez and Amharic into Latin for Rome and Avignon. Tamrat believed she had only "market words" of Latin until Ch.3's close.
- Zoser and Matteo: Arabic (Ch.11).
- Giovanni hears Ge'ez and Amharic as sound without sense.
- The veil speaks in its sources' tongues and cadences; only its quotation of Giovanni (Latin) is understood by the hall.
- **Ch.8, exact wording of Giovanni's question** [R2-mystery-08], said in Latin, turned in his chair, to Tamrat's face: **"Is she your wife, Father, or your sin?"** Angeska renders it mildly; Tamrat answers, via her, "that I am his wife, and that God has blessed it" (his thumb rubbing his palm). Also said in Ch.8 and quotable: "I feed Rome, Father. You are not Rome." (Angeska softens it; Tamrat inclines his head); "In Rome I am what Rome has."; "Justice is whatever the Church decrees. And the Church has decreed forty days."
- Ch.8: the first audience lasts about three hours (prime summons; Giovanni arrives after the dole; dismissed after sext). The embassy is lodged in the friary's **north rooms by the dormitory stair**, a guard on the door day and night, **water only**.
