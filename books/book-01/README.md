# THE BLACKFLAMES — Book I: *The Fragment Bearer*

*Lalibela, the Mediterranean, Rome and Avignon, c.1310 and 1325–1328.*

An embassy of four leaves the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela for Rome, carrying a letter from the King of Kings. One of them carries something more. A sea captain with a scholar's hands walks the deck with a candle that leans toward what it is looking for.
