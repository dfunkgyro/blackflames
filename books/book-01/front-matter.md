# THE BLACKFLAMES

## BOOK I — THE FRAGMENT BEARER

## FROM THE KEEPER'S CATALOGUE

*Rome, Yekatit ፲፰፻፹፱*

*Item the first. One sheet of vellum, unusually supple, scorched black at every edge and yet entire. I have taken its measure with my own hand, which is the only rule I trust, three times, and had three answers. The scorch does not flake; pressed, it gives, and is slow to come back. The script is one I was taught to read by my mother, who died in the hour I was born, and whom I have been carrying since. The veins of the hide are visible and do not sit still under the lamp. It smells of smoke, and under the smoke of salt, and under the salt of a hearth long cold. Provenance: Avignon, by way of Lalibela, by way of a sea I will not name. My mark, a spiral, is set in the lower margin of this leaf, as on every leaf of this book. I have kept worse things. I have not catalogued anything I loved.*

— B.
